Day 2 · Fri 8 Aug

Into the Rainforest

Similajau National Park — the big day
7:00 AM

Kopitiam breakfast + pack lunch

Cheap eats

The room rate has no breakfast, so eat at a local kopitiam — this is the morning for laksa Sarawak or kolo mee. Then buy bread, fruit and water at a supermarket for the park; food inside is limited and pricier.

💡 Packing your own park lunch is the single biggest saving of the trip.
Open in Maps →
🚗 45 min drive north
8:30 AM

Similajau National Park

RM10 entryAll morning

The heart of the trip. Jungle boardwalks, hornbills and macaques, and trails opening onto Golden Beach and Turtle Beach. Entry is only RM10 — walk the marked trails yourselves instead of hiring a guide and it stays cheap.

⚠️ Saltwater crocodiles live here — swim only where signs allow it. Bring water, repellent and sunscreen from town.

Where the savings come from
  • Car is collected at the airport — zero Grab or taxi fares all trip.
  • Airport lunch on Day 1 is unavoidable, so eat at KLIA first and keep the terminal spend small.
  • Eating at kopitiams and night markets instead of tourist restaurants.
  • Packing your own lunch for the park.
  • Skipping the RM85–110 river cruise — the bridge sunset is free.
  • Walking Similajau's marked trails instead of hiring a guide.
  • Beaches, esplanade, mosque, village and market all cost nothing.
